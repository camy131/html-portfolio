Jenny Cookie Recipe

Ingredients 材料 (25塊曲奇）

  • 有鹽牛油 （奶脂 90%）salted butter / unsalted butter(add 1g salt) 113g
  • 糖霜 Sifted Icing sugar 38g
  • 低筋麵粉 Sifted Cake Flour 113g
  • 粟粉 Sifted Cornstarch 40g
  • 全脂奶粉 Sifted Whole milk powder 5g
  • 鮮忌廉奶油 Whipping cream （奶脂 35%) 16g
  • 2 cups chocolate chips (or your favorite mix-ins)

Instructions

  1. In a stand mixer bowl, cream the butter, sifted both (Whole milk powder and Icing sugar) at a high speed until both ingredients are fully integrated. It should look a bit lighter in color and fluffy. Don’t over-cream the mixture, but fluffier the better! This took me 5 minutes.
  2. After that, add Whipping cream, add Sifted Flour and Cornstarch together, and then mix (use dough hood) it at a low speed for 5 mins.
  3. Remove the mixing bowl from the stand mixer, use a spatula to integrate well these ingredients into the butter mix. Don’t overmix the dough!
  4. Preheat the oven to 290°F with convection fan. Feel the dough and ensure that it’s smooth, and if not, add a bit of milk to ensure that it’s the right texture. Start with 1-2 tablespoons and add more as needed. Transfer the dough to a piping bag.
  5. Then, pipe the dough using a 1M open star tip onto a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. If the dough is too hard to pipe, you can add milk to it to make sure it’s the right consistency.
  6. Refrigerate the piped cookies for 15-20 minutes before baking. This will allow it to settle into its shape and prevent excess spreading when you bake it. The longer the better, so you can even refrigerate up to 40 minutes.
  7. Cook to 290°F for 35 minutes. Change to 325°F for 5 minutes
  8. Allow cookies to cool on the baking sheets for a few minutes before transferring them to wire racks to cool completely.

Enjoy!

Your delicious Jenny Cookies are ready to be enjoyed! Serve them fresh or store them in an airtight container.
